Track whether quantum readiness is improving or drifting.

Worsened

Private by design

Useful security intelligence without unnecessary exposure.

Local-first scanning Start with public TLS metadata and expand inside customer-controlled environments.
Trust receipts Every report explains what was used, what was not collected, and whether external AI was involved.
Private AI-ready path Future AI analysis should run locally, in a private cloud, or in air-gapped deployments for sensitive teams.

AI roadmap

Private today. AI-ready by design.

The current prototype does not send findings to external AI. The next AI layer should explain risk from sanitized CBOM metadata, then support local or customer-controlled model deployment when the product is ready.

Now Local CBOM reports from public TLS metadata. No external AI used.
Next Template-based readiness plans and evidence packs generated from CBOM findings.
Later Private AI summaries running locally, in customer cloud, or in air-gapped environments.
