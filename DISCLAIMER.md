# Disclaimer

Qyrion is a prototype and portfolio project. It is intended for education, experimentation, and early product exploration.

Qyrion is not:

- a full cybersecurity assessment
- a penetration test
- a compliance attestation
- legal advice
- cryptographic engineering advice
- proof that any system is secure or quantum-safe

The current scanner works with public TLS certificate metadata. Its findings are intentionally limited and should be reviewed by qualified security professionals before being used for business, legal, compliance, or production security decisions.

Post-quantum cybersecurity is an evolving field. Standards, vendor support, migration practices, and risk models may change over time.
