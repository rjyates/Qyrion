# Qyrion Education Strategy

## Core Message

Have a plan before you need one.

Quantum security is not about panic. It is about preparation.

Qyrion helps businesses understand where their cryptography lives, what could become vulnerable, and how to prepare before quantum risk becomes an emergency.

## Education Goal

Make quantum security important, understandable, and actionable for business leaders and technical teams.

Qyrion should become the company people trust to answer:

- What is quantum cybersecurity?
- Why does it matter to my business?
- What is at risk now?
- What should we do first?
- How do we prepare without exposing sensitive data?
- How do we make a plan before we need one?

## Tone

Qyrion should not sound like fear marketing.

Use:

- Calm urgency
- Plain English
- Practical examples
- Clear next steps
- Business relevance
- Technical credibility

Avoid:

- Doomsday language
- Overpromising
- Pretending quantum attacks are already breaking everything
- Making people feel embarrassed for not knowing
- Dense academic explanations before the business problem is clear

## Plain-English Explanation

Quantum computers could eventually break much of the public-key cryptography that protects today's internet, software, banking, healthcare, legal, government, and business systems.

The risk is not only future attacks. Sensitive encrypted data can be stolen now and decrypted later if it needs to stay confidential for many years.

The first step is not panic or instant replacement.

The first step is a plan.

That plan starts with a CBOM: a Cryptographic Bill of Materials.

## Why CBOM Matters

A company cannot protect cryptography it cannot see.

A CBOM helps answer:

- What cryptography do we use?
- Where do we use it?
- Which systems are quantum-vulnerable?
- Which data needs long-term protection?
- What should be migrated first?
- Which vendors create risk?
- What evidence can we show customers, auditors, and leadership?

## Audience Segments

### Executives

Message:

Quantum security is a business continuity and trust issue. You do not need to replace everything today, but you do need visibility and a migration plan.

Primary concern:

Risk, compliance, customer trust, cost, timing, and board-level preparedness.

### CISOs And Security Leaders

Message:

Qyrion helps you inventory cryptographic exposure, prioritize quantum risk, and build a defensible migration roadmap without sending sensitive security data to external AI.

Primary concern:

Visibility, prioritization, auditability, vendor risk, and safe execution.

### Engineering Teams

Message:

Qyrion turns cryptographic discovery into actionable technical records, reports, and remediation steps.

Primary concern:

Where crypto exists, what needs changing, and how to avoid breaking production systems.

### Compliance And Risk Teams

Message:

Qyrion helps create evidence that your organization understands quantum risk and has a preparation plan.

Primary concern:

Documentation, governance, customer questionnaires, audits, and policy alignment.

## Educational Video Strategy

Videos should be short, calm, and easy to understand.

The goal is not to prove how technical Qyrion is. The goal is to help a business owner, executive, or security leader understand enough to make a smart next decision.

Recommended format:

- 60 to 120 seconds each
- One concept per video
- Plain language
- Simple visuals
- No fear-based narration
- End with one practical takeaway

Website placement:

- Homepage explainer section
- Quantum Security 101 page
